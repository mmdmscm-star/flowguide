# Arm O decision rule — recorded BEFORE any call was made

Question: does letting the model understand the whole source before chunked
execution materially improve the chunked result?

## Arm validity (checked first; failure voids the arm, not the hypothesis)

- The orientation brief must contain ZERO record-specific values: no URL, email,
  phone, price or other specific detectable by the same `specifics()` extractor
  used for fabrication scoring. A brief that carries values is covert
  extraction, and the arm is invalid rather than successful.
- Cross-record leakage must be 0. Every specific appearing in an item must be
  present in the chunk text that produced it. A fact that reached an item from
  another part of the source is a failure even if the fact is true.

## PASS on senior living (the primary corpus)

Material improvement in at least one of:
- whole-item consistency across 3 repetitions: **+5 or more of 20** over the
  paired A control (A has measured 3-4/20 in every run to date);
- accepted/source-backed claim rate: **+3 percentage points or more**.

AND no material worsening, all against the paired A control:
- omissions: not more than +2;
- fabrication: stays 0;
- attribution/misbinding unresolved: stays 0;
- unauthorized private notes: stays 0;
- malformed responses: stays 0.

"Different prose" is not improvement. A change in description wording with no
movement in consistency or accepted rate is a null result.

## Controls

- Ice cream must stay essentially clean: accepted >= 99%, omissions 0,
  fabrication 0, leakage 0.
- Cross-vertical must not materially regress: accepted within 2 points of the
  paired A, fabrication 0, leakage 0.

## Stopping

This answers one question. No production change follows from any outcome, and
the review/rerun pass is not built in this round regardless of result.
