Top 15 Ice Cream Shops
in the San Francisco Bay Area 
Shop Directory
1. Mitchell's Ice Cream — San Francisco
Address
688 San Jose Ave, San Francisco, CA 94110
Phone
(415) 648-2300
Website
mitchellsicecream.com

 A family-run San Francisco institution since 1953, known for super-premium ice cream and a deep lineup of tropical and Filipino-inspired flavors, including its famous mango and ube.
Why it made the list: Longstanding Bay Area icon; featured in the San Francisco Chronicle's current Bay Area ice-cream guide.
2. Bi-Rite Creamery — San Francisco
Address
3692 18th St, San Francisco, CA 94110
Phone
(415) 626-5600
Website
biritemarket.com

 Mission District favorite across from Dolores Park, making small-batch ice cream, soft serve and frozen treats with seasonal ingredients. Salted caramel is a signature.
Why it made the list: A modern Bay Area classic and a recurring top pick in major local food guides.
3. Screamin' Mimi's — Sebastopol (Sonoma County)
Address
6902 Sebastopol Ave, Sebastopol, CA 95472
Phone
(707) 823-5902
Website
www.screaminmimisicecream.com

 Colorful Sebastopol destination making all-natural ice cream and sorbet with seasonal ingredients. The shop is especially known for Mimi's Mud and a lively, old-school ice-cream-shop feel.
Why it made the list: A Sonoma County institution with sustained local and national recognition.
4. Fentons Creamery — Oakland
Address
4226 Piedmont Ave, Oakland, CA 94611
Phone
(510) 658-7000
Website
www.fentonscreamery.com

 Founded in Oakland in 1894, Fentons combines a vintage ice cream parlor with a full restaurant. It is famous for handcrafted ice cream, oversized sundaes and its signature Black & Tan.
Why it made the list: 2026 Bay Area Parent East Bay reader-poll winner and one of the region's most historic creameries.
5. Garden Creamery — San Francisco
Address
3566 20th St, San Francisco, CA 94110
Phone
(808) 224-6626
Website
gardencreamery.com

 Mission District shop producing dense, small-batch ice cream from scratch with local organic dairy, plus coconut-based vegan options. Flavors often draw on Asian, Hawaiian and tropical ingredients.
Why it made the list: Highly rated independent shop with distinctive flavors and strong local-food sourcing.
6. Noble Folk Ice Cream & Pie Bar — Healdsburg (Sonoma County)
Address
116 Matheson St, Healdsburg, CA 95448
Phone
(707) 395-4426
Website
www.thenoblefolk.com

 A Healdsburg Plaza dessert shop pairing small-batch, inventive ice creams with house-made pies, macarons and other baked goods. Seasonal flavors and restrained sweetness are hallmarks.
Why it made the list: Featured in the Chronicle's Bay Area ice-cream coverage and a prominent Sonoma County destination.
7. Miharu Ice Cream — Oakland
Address
1951 Telegraph Ave, Oakland, CA 94612
Phone
(510) 922-8385
Website
www.miharuicecream.com

 Independent Oakland creamery built around internationally inspired, playful flavor pairings. The menu mixes familiar favorites with offbeat combinations and includes vegan choices.
Why it made the list: One of the strongest current customer-rated independent scoop shops in central Oakland.
8. Caravaggio Gelato Lab — Berkeley
Address
1797 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley, CA 94709
Phone
(510) 900-2401
Website
www.caravaggiogelato.com

 A North Berkeley gelato specialist with a science-minded approach to texture and ingredients. Expect Italian classics such as pistachio, stracciatella and gianduia alongside fruit-forward sorbets.
Why it made the list: Included in the Chronicle's current Bay Area best-ice-cream guide.
9. Tara's Organic Ice Cream — Berkeley
Address
3173 College Ave, Berkeley, CA 94705
Phone
(510) 655-5014
Website
tarasorganic.com

 Berkeley artisan shop centered on organic ingredients and an unusually broad rotating flavor library, from honey and cardamom to floral, herbal and fruit-driven combinations.
Why it made the list: Long-running East Bay favorite included in major local ice-cream roundups.
10. Philmore Creamery — San Francisco
Address
1840 Fillmore St, San Francisco, CA 94115
Phone
(415) 400-4076
Website
philmorecreamery.com

 Family-run Fillmore Street gelateria making gelato and sorbetto on site in small batches. It emphasizes Italian technique, local dairy and natural ingredients without artificial flavorings.
Why it made the list: Highly rated San Francisco gelato specialist with a strong artisan focus.
11. Sweet Scoops Homemade Ice Cream — Sonoma (Sonoma County)
Address
408 1st St E, Sonoma, CA 95476
Phone
(707) 721-1187
Website
www.sweetscoopsicecream.com

 Sonoma Plaza shop making ice cream on site with fresh and often local ingredients. Its rotating menu ranges from classics to unexpected seasonal flavors, with dairy-free options as well.
Why it made the list: The Chronicle calls it a Sonoma Plaza heavyweight; also recommended in Wine Country travel coverage.
12. Meadowlark Dairy — Pleasanton
Address
57 W Neal St, Pleasanton, CA 94566
Phone
(925) 846-2261
Website
www.meadowlarkdairy.com

 Century-old Pleasanton landmark best known for soft serve from a classic drive-through and walk-up window. Chocolate and vanilla anchor a rotating lineup of seasonal flavors.
Why it made the list: 2026 Bay Area Parent East Bay silver winner and a Chronicle-recommended historic Bay Area stop.
13. Cookiebar Scoop Shop — Alameda
Address
1608 Webster St, Alameda, CA 94501
Phone
(510) 521-1544
Website
cookiebarscoopshop.com

 Alameda's West End scoop shop known for smooth, creative ice creams, including Asian-inspired flavors such as egg tart alongside approachable classics.
Why it made the list: Strong local following and high customer ratings; a distinctive Alameda representative.
14. Angela's Organic Ice Cream — Petaluma (Sonoma County)
Address
134 Petaluma Blvd N, Petaluma, CA 94952
Phone
(707) 774-6567
Website
www.angelasicecream.com

 Downtown Petaluma artisan shop focused on small-batch organic ice cream, locally sourced ingredients and rotating creative flavors, with vegan and seasonal selections.
Why it made the list: Established Sonoma County artisan creamery with multiple North Bay locations.
15. Koolfi Creamery — San Francisco
Address
50 Fremont St, Suite 125, San Francisco, CA 94105
Phone
(415) 390-6210
Website
koolficreamery.com

 Indian-inspired creamery using organic dairy and real spices, fruits and house-made dessert mix-ins. Signature ideas include Klassic Koolfi, mango lassi and cardamom-forward flavors.
Why it made the list: Featured in current Chronicle/Eater coverage for distinctive Indian-inspired Bay Area ice cream.
