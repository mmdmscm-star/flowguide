# FlowGuide --- Project Context

*Read this document before making product recommendations or
architectural decisions.*

## What FlowGuide Is

FlowGuide is a communication platform.

It helps professionals transform information into communication.

The packet---not the AI---is the product.

## What FlowGuide Is Not

FlowGuide is not an AI product, a CRM, a website builder, or a document
editor. Those may support FlowGuide, but they are not what users value.

## The Core Model

``` text
Information (from anywhere)
        ↓
      Packet
        ↓
Communication (to anywhere)
```

Many inputs converge into one canonical packet.

That packet becomes the single source of truth.

Many renderers present that one packet.

## Current Stage

FlowGuide has moved beyond proving the concept.

The current objective is to make it excellent enough that professionals
outside the founder's own experience adopt it naturally.

Earlier decisions asked:

> Can we build this?

Today's decisions ask:

> Will this genuinely improve how professionals communicate?

## How We Build

Real workflows decide.

When real use exposes friction, solve the smallest problem that honestly
solves it.

## Design Philosophy

-   Preserve architecture.
-   Preserve simplicity.
-   Prefer additive improvements.
-   Avoid second sources of truth.
-   Avoid multiple ways to accomplish the same task.

## AI's Role

AI is one input among many.

Once the packet exists, the professional becomes its editor.

The packet belongs to the professional.

## Long-Term Vision

FlowGuide should become the easiest way for a professional to create and
share a polished packet of information, regardless of industry.

## Success

Success is when a professional confidently sends a FlowGuide packet
instead of an email because it better represents the quality of their
work.
